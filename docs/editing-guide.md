# Editing the Age of Creativity website

This guide is for anyone updating the site. You don't need to know any code.

Log in at `/wp-admin` on the site's address.

## Change the text on a page

1. Go to **Pages** in the left menu and click the page (for example *About*).
2. Click on any text and type. Use the **+** button to add a new paragraph, heading, image, list, button and so on.
3. Click **Save** (top right).

The short sentence shown in grey under each page's title is the page's **Excerpt**. Change it in the right-hand panel under *Page → Excerpt*.

To show a large picture under a page's title, set a **Featured image** in the same right-hand panel.

## Add a ready-made section

The site comes with sections that already look right, so you only have to replace the text:

1. Click **+** (top left), choose **Patterns**, then the **Age of Creativity** category.
2. Click one to add it: *Team members*, *Publication list*, *Report for download*, *Event*, *Contact details*, *Highlighted note* or *Text next to an image*.
3. Replace the example text. To change a photo, click it and choose **Replace**.

To add another person, publication or programme line, select an existing one and use **Duplicate** from the ⋮ menu in the small toolbar above it.

## Add a page or change the menu

1. **Pages → Add New Page**, write it and **Publish**.
2. To put it in the menu: **Appearance → Editor → Navigation**, then click **+** where you want it and pick the page. Drag items to reorder them. To place a page under *Output*, drag it into that item.

The footer menu shows the same links automatically.

## The timeline on the front page

The people on the timeline are not on a page. They have their own section in the left menu, **Creators**.

- **Add a person:** Creators → Add creator. Fill in the name, year born, year died (leave empty if alive), pick a field, write one or two sentences, and tick who they're connected to. Click **Publish**.
- **Edit or remove a person:** Creators → All creators, then click the name, or hover and choose *Trash*.
- **Fields and their colours:** Creators → Fields. Each field has a colour and a list of eras (one per line, for example `Baroque, 1600, 1750`). The eras appear under the timeline when that field is selected.
- **Years shown and the general periods:** Creators → Timeline settings.

A connection only needs ticking on one of the two people; the line is drawn both ways.

The big title and the question on the front page come from **Settings → General** (*Site Title* and *Tagline*). The text under the timeline is the *Home* page, which you edit like any other page.

## News posts

**Posts → Add New Post** publishes a dated news item. Posts are listed on the posts page if one is set under *Settings → Reading*.

## Colours and fonts

**Appearance → Editor → Styles** changes colours and type sizes across the whole site. The field colours on the timeline are set under *Creators → Fields* instead.

## If something goes missing

**Appearance → Starter content → Add missing starter content** puts back any of the standard pages, the menu or the fields if they were deleted. It never changes or removes anything that exists.
